Privacy Policy

UK AND ASSOCIATES LLC respects the privacy of our customers and is committed to handling personal information and GPS vehicle tracking data responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we may collect, how that information may be used, how information may be disclosed, and the measures we take to protect customer information.

Effective Date: August 25,2026

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services requested, UK AND ASSOCIATES LLC may collect information including:

  • Customer name
  • Business name
  • Mailing or service address
  • Telephone number
  • Email address
  • Vehicle year, make, model, and related vehicle information
  • GPS tracker model and device identification information
  • GPS location and vehicle movement data
  • Account and service usage information
  • Billing and payment-related information
  • Technical and support information related to services we provide

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing GPS tracking and monitoring services
  • Installing and configuring technology equipment
  • Supporting customer accounts
  • Providing technical support
  • Maintaining service and system security
  • Processing payments and maintaining business records
  • Communicating with customers about service requests
  • Resolving technical problems or disputes
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations

3. GPS Location Data

Customers using our GPS vehicle tracking services may generate vehicle location, movement, trip, speed, event, and related tracking information.

GPS tracking data associated with a customer account is treated as customer data and is stored and processed for purposes related to providing and administering the GPS tracking service.

UK AND ASSOCIATES LLC does not sell customer GPS location information.

GPS tracking services are provided only when the customer represents that they own the vehicle or otherwise have lawful authority to authorize installation and tracking.

4. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

UK AND ASSOCIATES LLC does not sell customer personal information.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary:

  • With the customer's authorization
  • To service providers supporting our business operations
  • To technology or hosting providers supporting the services we provide
  • To process payments or provide customer support
  • To comply with applicable law
  • In response to valid legal process

Service providers receiving customer information should only receive information reasonably necessary to perform the service for which they are engaged.

5. Law Enforcement and Legal Requests

UK AND ASSOCIATES LLC does not voluntarily provide customer GPS tracking records, account information, or location history to law enforcement agencies, private investigators, employers, family members, or other third parties except as authorized by the customer, required by law, or otherwise legally permitted.

Information may be disclosed in response to legally valid process, which may include:

  • A court order
  • A search warrant
  • A grand jury subpoena
  • A civil subpoena that legally requires disclosure
  • Other legally valid process

Unless prohibited by law, court order, warrant, or applicable emergency circumstances, UK AND ASSOCIATES LLC may make reasonable efforts to notify an affected customer before disclosing customer information.

The Company may respond to legally authorized emergency requests involving circumstances such as imminent death, serious bodily injury, or significant property damage.

6. Data Security

UK AND ASSOCIATES LLC uses reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards intended to protect customer information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction.

Security measures may include access controls, account credentials, server and network protections, software updates, backups, and other reasonable safeguards appropriate to the services being provided.

No electronic transmission, computer system, cellular network, internet service, or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Customer Account Security

Customers are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of usernames, passwords, and other credentials used to access GPS tracking or other customer systems.

Customers should notify UK AND ASSOCIATES LLC promptly if they believe their account credentials or service access have been compromised.

8. Data Retention

Customer, account, service, and GPS tracking information may be retained as reasonably necessary for:

  • Providing active services
  • Business operations
  • System backups
  • Security purposes
  • Dispute resolution
  • Legal compliance
  • Record preservation obligations

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information, service requirements, technical limitations, customer agreements, and applicable legal requirements.

9. Customer Requests

Customers may contact UK AND ASSOCIATES LLC to request access to certain account information or to request correction of inaccurate customer information.

Requests involving GPS history, account deletion, data export, or other records may be subject to identity verification, technical availability, retention requirements, legal obligations, and the terms of the customer's service agreement.

10. Third-Party Services

Some services provided by UK AND ASSOCIATES LLC may depend on third-party technology, cellular networks, hosting providers, equipment manufacturers, payment processors, mapping services, or other service providers.

These third-party services may operate under their own privacy, security, and service terms.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

UK AND ASSOCIATES LLC may update this Privacy Policy as our services, technology, business practices, or legal requirements change.

Updated versions may be posted on this website with a revised effective date.
