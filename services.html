Our Technology Services

UK AND ASSOCIATES LLC provides practical technology solutions designed to improve security, visibility, performance, and connectivity for individuals and businesses throughout the Philadelphia area.

From GPS vehicle tracking and surveillance systems to computer upgrades and network installation, we provide professional installation, configuration, and support based on each customer's specific needs.

GPS Vehicle Tracking & Monitoring

Our GPS tracking solutions help customers maintain greater visibility of personal vehicles, business vehicles, and light commercial fleets.

Depending on the selected equipment and service configuration, available features may include:

  • Real-time vehicle location
  • Trip and route history
  • Geofencing
  • Towing and unauthorized movement alerts
  • Tracker disconnect or tamper alerts
  • Overspeed notifications
  • Driver-behavior monitoring
  • Backup battery operation
  • Ignition and movement detection
  • Remote access to tracking information

We offer options for both simple installations and more advanced professional hardwired installations, depending on the vehicle and customer requirements.
