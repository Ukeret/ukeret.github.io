Residential & Commercial Security Camera Solutions

Residential Security Cameras

Security cameras can provide greater visibility around a home and help document activity at important locations.

Common residential coverage areas include:

  • Front and rear entrances
  • Driveways
  • Garages
  • Backyards
  • Side walkways
  • Common or parking areas

We work with the customer to balance useful coverage, camera placement, appearance, and available cabling routes.

Commercial Security Cameras

We provide surveillance solutions for small businesses, offices, shops, warehouses, garages, and other commercial locations.

Common commercial coverage areas include:

  • Building entrances and exits
  • Customer areas
  • Parking lots
  • Inventory and storage areas
  • Work areas
  • Exterior perimeter locations

Camera placement is planned around the customer's property, risk areas, existing equipment, and surveillance goals.

Camera Placement & Coverage Planning

A good surveillance system depends on more than the number of cameras installed. Camera placement, viewing angle, lighting, mounting height, and image detail all affect how useful recorded video will be.

During planning, we may evaluate:

  • Entrances and exits
  • Vehicle and parking areas
  • Blind spots
  • Interior and exterior lighting
  • Camera mounting locations
  • Cabling routes
  • Recorder location
  • Monitor location
  • Network access for remote viewing

Important Security Camera Information

Security cameras can improve property visibility and provide useful recorded evidence, but no surveillance system can guarantee the prevention of theft, vandalism, intrusion, or other incidents.

Camera performance can be affected by lighting, weather, network availability, equipment condition, storage capacity, camera angle, obstructions, and other environmental or technical factors.

Customers are responsible for using surveillance equipment in accordance with applicable privacy, recording, workplace, and property laws.
