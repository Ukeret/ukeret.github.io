About UK AND ASSOCIATES LLC

UK AND ASSOCIATES LLC is a Philadelphia-based technology services company providing practical technology solutions for individuals, homes, vehicles, and businesses.

Our services include GPS vehicle tracking and monitoring, security camera installation, computer upgrades and repair, network installation, and related technical support.

Our goal is simple: understand what the customer needs, recommend an appropriate solution, install and configure the technology properly, and provide dependable support.

What We Do

Vehicle Technology

We provide GPS vehicle tracking and monitoring solutions designed to improve vehicle visibility, security awareness, and fleet management capabilities.

  • Vehicle monitoring solutions
  • Geofencing and movement alerts
  • Professional hardwired installations
  • Simple installation options

Security & Surveillance

We install and configure residential and commercial surveillance systems based on the customer's property, coverage needs, and security goals.

Professional Service With Flexibility

Every customer and project is different. A homeowner installing a few security cameras may have very different requirements from a business expanding its network or a vehicle owner looking for GPS tracking.

We approach each project individually and consider factors such as:

  • Customer goals
  • Existing equipment
  • Installation environment
  • Budget
  • Security and privacy considerations
  • Future expansion
  • Reliability and maintenance

This allows us to recommend solutions that are appropriate for the customer's actual needs rather than taking a one-size-fits-all approach.

Privacy & Responsible Technology

Some of the technologies we work with, particularly GPS tracking and surveillance systems, can involve sensitive information.

UK AND ASSOCIATES LLC believes that these technologies should be used responsibly and lawfully.

GPS tracking services are provided only when the customer represents that they have lawful authority to authorize tracking. Customer information and GPS tracking data are handled according to our service agreements, privacy practices, and applicable law.

We also encourage customers to use security cameras and other monitoring technology in accordance with applicable privacy, property, employment, and recording laws.
