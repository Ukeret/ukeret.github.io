Installation Options

Easy-Install GPS Tracking

Designed for customers who want reliable vehicle tracking with a simpler installation and minimal vehicle wiring.

Depending on the selected hardware, this option may provide:

  • Real-time tracking
  • Towing and movement detection
  • Disconnect alerts
  • Geofencing
  • Overspeed monitoring
  • Internal backup battery

This option may be suitable for personal vehicles and customers who prefer a faster installation.

Professional Hardwired GPS Tracking

Designed for customers who want a more permanent, professionally installed tracking solution with additional vehicle integration options.

Depending on the selected hardware and installation, available capabilities may include:

  • Dedicated ignition detection
  • Hidden vehicle installation
  • Digital and analog inputs
  • Additional sensor integration
  • Driver-behavior monitoring
  • Fleet and business applications

Advanced features are evaluated based on the vehicle, equipment, and customer's specific requirements.

Monitoring Service

Customers with an active monitoring service receive access to an approved GPS tracking platform where available vehicle information can be viewed securely.

Depending on the service configuration, customers may be able to view:

  • Current vehicle location
  • Recent trips and travel history
  • Vehicle status information
  • Geofence activity
  • Movement and towing events
  • Speed-related events
  • Tracker connectivity information

Monitoring features and data availability vary based on the equipment, connectivity, service plan, and platform configuration.
